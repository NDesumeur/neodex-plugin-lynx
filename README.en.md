# Eagle Eye

A pixel art spot-the-difference game: two almost identical pictures, find what has changed before time runs out.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Controls

- **Mouse**: click a difference, in the left or right picture.
- **Keyboard**: the **arrows** move the cursor, **Enter** to point.
- **H**: a hint (costs 15 seconds).
- **P** or **Esc**: pause.

## How it works

- On the right, a few things have changed: something has **disappeared**, **changed colour** or **faces the other way**.
- Each difference you find is circled on both pictures.
- A **wrong click** costs 5 seconds. You have **a minute and a half** per level.
- The more time you have left at the end, the more **stars** you earn (up to 3).
- Every level draws a new scene: a **garden**, a **beach**, a **city at night**, a **forest**, the **seabed**, **space** or a **winter** landscape. There are 5 differences at first, then 6, then 7.

## Stats

The Bests panel keeps the **level reached**, **stars**, **differences found** and the number of **games**. The game resumes at the level reached.

## Data and sync

Your bests are saved by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs if sync is on.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Eagle Eye → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
